What is WebsiteDeals Support?

WebsiteDeals Support is the customer assistance center for basic questions, website help, package information, account assistance and direct support.

What can Usha.ai help me with?

Usha.ai is designed for first-level basic Q/A, common website questions, package information, account questions and general guidance.

Can I talk to a real person?

Yes. If your question needs human assistance, you can contact the WebsiteDeals Support Team directly.

Can support help with website problems?

Basic website-related questions can be handled through the support workflow. More technical issues can be escalated to the development team.

How does Usha.ai connect me to the team?

Usha.ai works as the first support layer. When a question requires human assistance, the user can move from AI support to the appropriate WebsiteDeals team member.
